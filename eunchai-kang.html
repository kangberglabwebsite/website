Group leader

Eunchai Kang

Developmental neuroscientist · University of Aberdeen

Eunchai leads research in the Kang/Berg Lab on human cortical development, neurogenesis and stem cell-derived brain models, with a particular focus on how environmental and inflammatory signals influence the developing brain.

Research

Eunchai studies the cellular and molecular mechanisms that shape human brain development, with a particular interest in cortical neurogenesis and the response of developing neural tissue to environmental and inflammatory signals.

Her work combines human stem cell-derived organoids and assembloids with ex vivo human fetal brain models to investigate how neural stem cells build organised tissue and how interactions among neural and non-neural cell types influence developmental outcomes.

Research interests

  • Human cortical development and neurogenesis
  • Brain organoids, cerebroids and assembloids
  • Maternal inflammation and IL-17A signalling
  • Neuroimmune interactions during development
  • Human experimental models of neurological disease
